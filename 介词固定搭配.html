时间介词时间介词 — at / on / in

at — 点时间

at 7:00 / noon / night / midnight

on — 天时间

on Monday / May 1st
⚠ on a cold morning

in — 段时间

in 2024 / spring / June / the morning

1
He arrived ___ a cold winter morning.
oninat
✅ 具体某天的早晨→on！
❌ 具体某天早中晚→on！
2
We have lunch ___ noon.
atonin
✅ at noon固定搭配！
❌ noon用at！
💡 口诀
"at点on天in月年，具体某天早中晚用on"

地点介词

in 大地方（城市/国家）
at 小地方（车站/门口）
arrive in Shanghai / arrive at the station

动词+介词高频

agree with / belong to
care about / depend on
insist on / succeed in
suffer from / lead to

3
He finally succeeded ___ passing the exam.
inonat
✅ succeed in doing→in！
❌ succeed in doing！
4
The problem ___ serious consequences.
led toled inled on
✅ lead to→导致！
❌ lead to=导致！
