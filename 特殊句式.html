MODULE 1

🔄 倒装句

部分倒装 + 完全倒装 · 高考核心句式
M1 · 部分倒装部分倒装 — 把助动词/情态动词提到主语前
部分倒装 = 助动词/情态动词/be + 主语 + 谓语

① 否定词在句首

Never have I seen such a beautiful sunset.

我从未见过如此美丽的日落。

Never/Hardly/Seldom/Not until/No sooner/By no means

📋 更多例子
Not only does he sing, but he also dances. 他不仅唱歌还跳舞
Hardly had I arrived when it rained. 我刚到就下雨了

② Only + 状语在句首

Only then did I realize my mistake.

只有那时我才意识到错误。

Only + 状语(非主语) → 部分倒装

📋 更多例子
Only in this way can we solve the problem. 只有这样才能解决问题
Only when you try will you know. 只有试了你才知道

③ so/neither/nor 句首

He is tall, and so am I.

他很高，我也是。

So + 助动词 + 主语 = ……也

📋 更多例子
She didn't go, neither did I. 她没去我也没去
He can swim, so can she. 他会游泳她也会

④ not only...but also

Not only does he teach, but he also writes books.

他不仅教书还写书。

Not only 在句首 → 部分倒装

📋 更多例子
Not only is she beautiful, but also smart. 她不仅漂亮还聪明
Not until he came back did I leave. 直到他回来我才离开
"否定在首半倒装"|"Only+状也半倒"|"so/neither/not only同样"

M1 · 完全倒装完全倒装 — 整个谓语提到主语前
完全倒装 = 谓语 + 主语（主语是名词，不是代词）

① 地点状语在句首

Here comes the bus.

公交车来了。

Here/There + 不及物动词 + 名词主语

📋 更多例子
There goes the bell. 铃响了
In the room sits an old man. 房间里坐着一位老人

② 表语/分词在句首

Gone are the days when we played together.

我们一起玩耍的日子一去不复返了。

分词/形容词/介词短语在句首

📋 更多例子
Present at the meeting were all the leaders. 所有领导都出席了
Standing by the door is a policeman. 门口站着一个警察

③ 直接引语 + said he

"I love you," said the boy.

"我爱你"男孩说。

直接引语在前，said + 主语（名词）

📋 更多例子
"Hello," whispered she. "你好"她低声说
但："Hello," he said.（代词不倒装） undefined
💡 口诀
"地点在首全倒装，Here comes / There goes 最经典"

❌ Here + come + 名词主语 → 完全倒装！

MODULE 2

💡 强调句·省略句

It is...that... · 省略句 · 高考必考
M2 · 强调句强调句 — It is/was ... that/who ...必考句式
结构：It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 其余

强调主语

It was Tom who broke the window.

是Tom打破了窗户。

强调人 → that/who 均可

📋 更多例子
It is Mary that/who helps me most. 是Mary帮我最多
It was the book that changed my life. 是那本书改变了我

强调状语

It was in the park that we first met.

是在公园里我们第一次相遇。

强调地点/时间/方式状语 → 只能用that

📋 更多例子
It was yesterday that I received the letter. 是昨天我收到信
It is because of you that I succeed. 是因为你我成功

强调句 vs 定语从句

It was in this house that I was born.（强调句）
It was this house where I was born.（定语从句）

就是在这间房子里我出生了。

区别：去掉 It is...that 后句子完整 → 强调句

📋 更多例子
It was at 8:00 that we started. 是8点我们开始的
去掉框架"At 8:00 we started"仍然完整 undefined
"It is...that/who框"|"强调人可用who"|"去掉框架仍完整"
M2 · 强调句强调句 专项练习4题
1
It was in the library ___ I met her for the first time.
thatwherewhich
✅ 强调地点状语 → 只能用 that！去掉框架 "In the library I met her" 完整。
❌ 强调状语 → that，不是 where！
2
___ was he that broke the window?
WhoWhatWhich
✅ 对主语提问 → Who was it that...?
❌ 强调句疑问式 → Who was it that...?
3
It ___ Mike and John who helped me yesterday.
waswereis
✅ It is/was 永远用单数 → 不管被强调部分！
❌ It is/was 不被强调部分影响，永远单数！
4
Was it in 2020 ___ you graduated?
thatwhenwhich
✅ 强调时间状语→that！不算去掉框架判断。
❌ 强调时间状语→that！
M2 · 省略句省略句 — 避免重复，简洁表达

① 状语从句省略

When (I was) walking in the park, I met her.

在公园散步时遇到了她。

when/while/if/unless + doing/done（主语和be省略）

📋 更多例子
If (it is) possible, call me. 如果可能打我电话
Though (he was) tired, he kept working. 虽然累他继续工作

② 不定式省略to

He made me (to) cry.

他让我哭了。

make/let/have/see/watch + sb + do（省略to）

📋 更多例子
I saw him enter the room. 我看见他进了房间
She let me go. 她让我走

③ 比较状语从句省略

He runs faster than I (do/run).

他比我跑得快。

than/as 后面省略重复部分

📋 更多例子
She is as tall as you (are). 她跟你一样高
I like it more than (I like) him. 我喜欢它胜过喜欢他

④ if/when + 形容词

I'll go if (it is) necessary.

如果有必要我就去。

if/when + 形容词（省去主语+be）

📋 更多例子
Correct mistakes if (there is) any. 如果有错请改正
When (you are) in trouble, ask for help. 遇到困难时求助
"状从省略主+be"|"make/let/see省to"|"比较从句省重复"
M2 · 综合练强调句·省略句 综合练习4题
5

ItThatThis
✅ It was not until...that 句型！强调时间。
❌ not until 的强调句型 → It was not until...that！
6
When ___ (ask) about his plan, he kept silent.
askedaskingwas asked
✅ When + 过去分词(asked) → 省略了 he was！
❌ 状语从句省略：When (he was) asked → asked！
7
He is taller than ___ (I/me).
Imemy
✅ than 是连词 → I = I am！正式英语用 I。
❌ than 后是省略从句 → 用主格 I！
8
It is the factories ___ pour waste water into the river.
thatwherewho
✅ 强调主语 factories → that！不以人/物区分。
❌ 强调主语 → that 通用！
MODULE 3

❓ 反义疑问句

前肯后否 · 前否后肯 · 特殊形式
M3 · 反义疑问反义疑问句 — 基本规则
核心公式：陈述句 + 简短反问（相反形式）

① 前肯后否

He is a student, isn't he?

他是一个学生，不是吗？

陈述句肯定 → 反问否定

📋 更多例子
You like coffee, don't you? 你喜欢咖啡不是吗
They will come, won't they? 他们会来不是吗

② 前否后肯

She isn't happy, is she?

她不开心，是吗？

陈述句否定 → 反问肯定

📋 更多例子
He never smokes, does he? 他从不吸烟是吗
Nothing happened, did it? 什么都没发生是吗
💡 口诀
"前肯后否，前否后肯；人称时态与前面保持一致"
1
You like pop music, ___?
don't youdo youaren't you
✅ 前肯(like)后否，助动词用do→don't you！
❌ like是实义动词→反问用do/don't！
M3 · 反义疑问反义疑问句 — 特殊形式必考
陈述句特征反问部分例句
I amaren't I?I am right, aren't I?
Let's（包括对方）shall we?Let's go, shall we?
Let us（不包括对方）will you?Let us go, will you?
祈使句（肯定）will you / won't you?Open the door, will you?
祈使句（否定）will you?Don't be late, will you?
I think/believe + 从句从句反向I think he is right, isn't he?
must（必须）needn't?You must go, needn't you?
must（推测）依时态定He must be tired, isn't he?
had betterhadn't?You'd better go, hadn't you?
used todidn't / usedn't?He used to smoke, didn't he?
2
I am your friend, ___?
aren't Iamn't Iam I
✅ I am → aren't I！特殊规则。
❌ I am的反问 → aren't I！没有 amn't I！
3
Let's have a rest, ___?
shall wewill youdon't we
✅ Let's 包括对方 → shall we！
❌ Let's → shall we！Let us → will you！
M3 · 专项练反义疑问句 专项练习5题

❌ 否定祈使句 → will you！

❌ I think引导 → 反问从句部分！

❌ had better → hadn't！

MODULE 4

📌 There be · it 句型

There be 存在句 · it 形式主语/形式宾语
M4 · There beThere be 存在句 — "有"

There be 就近原则

There is a book and two pens.
There are two pens and a book.

有一本书和两支笔。

be 动词与最近的主语一致

📋 更多例子
There seems to be a problem. 似乎有个问题
There happened to be a doctor nearby. 碰巧附近有个医生

There be 变体

There stands a tower on the hill.
There lived an old man in the village.

山上矗立着一座塔。

stand/lie/live/exist/remain 可替代 be

📋 更多例子
There lies a river in front of the house. 房子前面有一条河
There remains a question to be solved. 还有一个问题要解决
1
There ___ a desk and three chairs in the room.
isarebe
✅ 就近原则 → a desk单数 → is！
❌ There be 就近原则 → 看最近主语！
M4 · it句型it 形式主语 / 形式宾语

形式主语

It is important to learn English.
→ To learn English is important.

学英语很重要。

真正主语（不定式/从句）置后

📋 更多例子
It is no use crying over spilt milk. 覆水难收
It is said that he is a genius. 据说他是天才

形式宾语

I find it hard to believe him.
→ I find to believe him hard.

我觉得很难相信他。

find/think/consider/make + it + adj + to do/that

📋 更多例子
I make it a rule to get up early. 我习惯早起
We consider it necessary that he go. 我们认为他去有必要

it 表时间/天气/距离

It is 8 o'clock.
It is sunny today.
It is 5 miles from here.

现在8点。今天晴天。离这5英里。

it 作无人称主语

📋 更多例子
It takes 2 hours to get there. 到那要两小时
It has been 3 years since I saw him. 三年没见他了
2
I consider ___ important to keep the environment clean.
itthatthis
✅ 形式宾语 → it！真正宾语是后面的 to do。
❌ 形式宾语 → find/think/consider + it！
M4 · 专项练There be / it 句型 专项练习4题
3

ThereItThat
✅ There is no doubt that → 固定句型！
❌ There is no doubt that → 固定！不是 It。
4

ItThereThat
✅ It is + 时间 + since → 固定句型！
❌ 时间+since → 用 It！
5

areisbe
✅ a lot of people → 复数 → are！
❌ people 是复数 → are！
6

itthatthis
✅ take it for granted that → 固定！it形式宾语。
❌ take it for granted that → it 形式宾语！
