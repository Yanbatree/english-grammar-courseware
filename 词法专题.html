MODULE 1

📚 冠词·名词·主谓一致

a/an/the/零冠词 · 可数不可数 · 主谓一致三大原则
M1 · 冠词冠词 a / an / the — 三兄弟各司其职

a — 一个（泛指）

I saw a dog.

我看到一只狗。（不特定哪只）

辅音音素前用 a

an — 一个（元音前）

She is an honest girl.

她是个诚实的女孩。

⚠ 元音音素前用 an，不是元音字母！hour/honest 用 an

the — 那个（特指）

The dog over there is mine.

那边的那只狗是我的。

双方都知道的、上文提过的、独一无二的

💡 秒记
"a/an泛指 the特指，元音音素用an不用a"
1
I need ___ hour to finish this work.
aanthe
✅ hour [aʊər] 以元音音素开头 → an！
❌ hour 的 h 不发音，元音开头 → an！
2
___ sun rises in the east.
AAnThe
✅ sun 是独一无二的事物 → the！
❌ 独一无二的事物前用 the！
M1 · 冠词+名词零冠词 + 名词可数不可数

零冠词（什么都不加）

① 复数泛指：Dogs are loyal.
② 三餐/球类/学科：have lunch, play basketball
③ 专有名词：China, Monday

📋 更多例子
by bus（交通方式，零冠词）坐公交
in hospital（住院）≠ in the hospital（在医院里）

名词可数 vs 不可数

可数：a book → two books
不可数：water, advice, information
⚠ 不可数名词不加 a/an，没有复数

📋 更多例子
a piece of advice（一条建议）advice不可数，用piece量化
furniture, equipment, news 都是不可数
3
He gave me ___ good advice.
aan/
✅ advice 不可数 → 不加冠词！
❌ advice 不可数名词，不能加 a/an！
4
They often play ___ football after school.
athe/
✅ 球类运动前不加冠词 → 零冠词！
❌ play + 球类 → 零冠词！
M1 · 主谓一致主谓一致 — 三大原则高频失分点

① 语法一致

主语单数 → 谓语单数
He likes music.
They like music.

最基本的原则

② 意义一致

The police are coming.
The family is big.
⚠ police/people/cattle 永远复数

③ 就近原则

Either you or he is wrong.
Not only they but also she likes it.

⚠ either...or / neither...nor / not only...but also

5
The police ___ searching for the thief.
isarewas
✅ police 永远复数 → are！
❌ police 是集合名词，永远用复数！
6
Neither he nor I ___ going to the party.
amisare
✅ 就近原则 → I 用 am！neither...nor 看最近的主语。
❌ neither...nor 就近原则 → I 决定用 am！
"语法看单复"|"意义看集合"|"就近看最近"
M1 · 主谓一致主谓一致 — 高频考点

就远原则

The girl together with her friends is here.
Nobody but Tom and Jack was late.

with/along with/together with/but/except → 看前面的主语

数量词作主语

Two hours is enough.（时间/距离/金钱 → 单数）
Three fourths of the water is polluted.
Three fourths of the students are boys.

⚠ 分数/百分数 + of → 看 of 后面的名词

7
The teacher with his students ___ going to the museum.
isarewere
✅ with 是就远原则 → 看 teacher 单数 → is！
❌ with his students 不看，看前面的 teacher → is！
8
Ten dollars ___ not a big deal for him.
isarewere
✅ 金钱作主语视为单数 → is！
❌ 金钱/时间/距离作主语用单数！

❌ 独一无二的事物 → the！

✅ maths 学科名 → 单数！以s结尾的学科名作单数。
❌ maths虽以s结尾但是单数！学科名。

athe/
✅ by + 交通工具 → 零冠词！
❌ by + 交通方式不加冠词！
4

isarebe
✅ 就近原则 → the girl 单数 → is！
❌ either...or 看最近的主语 → girl！

"独一无二用the"|"学科/球类零冠词"|"就近就远看仔细"

MODULE 2

🔤 代词·形容词副词

不定代词辨析 · 多形容词排序 · 比较级最高级
M2 · 代词不定代词辨析 — some/any, none/nothing, other/another

some vs any

some：肯定句 / 委婉请求
I have some questions.
Would you like some tea?（委婉）
any：否定/疑问句
I don't have any money.

none vs nothing

none：指代人或物（数量为零）
None of them came.
nothing：指代物（什么也没有）
There is nothing in the box.

other / the other / another

another：再一个（三者以上）
Can I have another chance?
the other：两者中的另一个
One is red, the other is blue.

either / neither / both / all

both：两者都 | either：两者任一
neither：两者都不 | all：三者及以上都
⚠ neither...nor 谓语就近

1
There are two apples. One is red, ___ is green.
the otheranotherother
✅ 两个中的另一个 → the other！
❌ 两者中的"另一个"用 the other！
M2 · 形容词多形容词排序 — "美小圆旧黄，法国木书房"
多个形容词修饰名词时的顺序：限定词 → 描绘 → 大小 → 形状 → 年龄 → 颜色 → 国籍 → 材料 → 名词

口诀：美小圆旧黄，法国木书房

a beautiful small round old yellow French wooden table
美(beautiful)小(small)圆(round)旧(old)黄(yellow)法国(French)木(wooden)书桌

⚠ 高考常见考法

① She has long black hair. ✓（大小+颜色）
② a Chinese famous writer ✗
  → a famous Chinese writer ✓（描绘+国籍）

2
It's a ___ table. (wooden / nice / round)
nice round woodenround nice woodenwooden nice round
✅ 描绘(nice)→形状(round)→材料(wooden)！
❌ 描绘→形状→材料 → nice round wooden！
💡 口诀
"美小圆旧黄，法国木书房" — 描述→大小→形状→新旧→颜色→国籍→材料
M2 · 比较级形容词/副词比较级 & 最高级

规则变化

单音节：tall → taller → tallest
辅+y：easy → easier → easiest
多音节：beautiful → more beautiful → most beautiful

不规则变化

good/well → better → best
bad/badly → worse → worst
many/much → more → most
little → less → least
far → farther/further

必考句型

① 比较级+than: He is taller than me.
② as...as: She is as tall as her sister.
③ the+比较级, the+比较级
The more you read, the better you write.

3
Of the two boys, Tom is ___ (tall).
the tallerthe tallesttaller
✅ 两者比较用 the + 比较级！
❌ of the two → 两者 → the + 比较级！
4
China is becoming ___ (strong) than ever before.
strongermore strongstrongest
✅ strong 单音节+er！more用于多音节词。
❌ strong单音节→stronger，不是more strong！
M2 · 比较级比较级易错陷阱
5

✓ 正确taller → tallestthe → a
✅ 两者之间的比较 → the + 比较级 → 正确！
❌ 两者之间用 the + 比较级，不是最高级！
6
This book is ___ more interesting than that one.
muchverymore
✅ 修饰比较级用 much/far/even/a lot → much more！
❌ very 不能修饰比较级！用 much/far/even！
7
He is one of the ___ (good) students in our class.
bestbettergood
✅ one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词！
❌ one of the 后用最高级！
8
The population of China is larger than ___ of Japan.
thatitone
✅ 比较结构中代替前面名词 → that（单数）！
❌ 比较结构中用 that/those 代替前面名词！
"比较级+than"|"the+比较级,the+比较级"|"much/far修饰比较级"

🔗 介词·固定搭配

时间介词 · 地点介词 · 动词+介词 · 完形填空高频搭配
M3 · 介词时间介词 — at / on / in

at — 点时间

at 7:00 / noon / night / midnight / the moment
具体时刻、正午、午夜

on — 天时间

on Monday / May 1st / a rainy day / the morning of June 1st
⚠ 具体某天的早中晚用 on！

in — 段时间

in 2024 / spring / June / the morning
年、月、季节、泛指的早中晚

1
He arrived ___ a cold winter morning.
oninat
✅ 具体某天的早晨 → on！a cold winter morning 是具体一天。
❌ 具体某天(有形容词修饰)的早中晚 → on！
2
We usually have lunch ___ noon.
atonin
✅ noon 固定用 at！at noon/night/midnight。
❌ at noon 是固定搭配！
💡 口诀
"at点on天in月年，具体某天早中晚用on"
M3 · 介词地点介词 + 动词固定搭配完形高频

地点介词

in 大地方（城市/国家）
at 小地方（车站/门口）
on 表面上 / 在……边上
⚠ arrive in (大城市) / arrive at (小地方)

完形高频动词+介词

agree with 同意  |  belong to 属于
care about 关心  |  depend on 依靠
insist on 坚持  |  succeed in 成功
suffer from 遭受  |  lead to 导致

3
He finally succeeded ___ passing the exam.
inonat
✅ succeed in doing → in！固定搭配。
❌ succeed in doing sth 固定搭配！
4
The problem ___ (lead to) serious consequences.
led toled inled on
✅ lead to 导致！固定短语。
❌ lead to = 导致！

MODULE 4

🎯 情态动词

情态表推测 · 情态 + have done · 高考核心考点
M4 · 情态动词情态动词基本用法 — 能力/许可/义务

can / could — 能力/许可

I can swim.（能力）
Could I use your phone?（委婉）
could 也可表过去的能力

may / might — 许可/可能

May I come in?（许可）
It might rain later.（较小的可能）
might 可能性比 may 小

must / have to — 必须

You must finish it today.（主观）
I have to go now.（客观）
⚠ mustn't = 禁止 ≠ 不必（needn't）

should / ought to — 应该

You should study harder.
You ought to apologize.
should = ought to（ought to 更正式）

1
You ___ smoke here. It's a hospital.
mustn'tneedn'tcan't
✅ mustn't = 禁止！医院禁止吸烟。
❌ mustn't = 禁止 ≠ needn't(不必)！
💡 秒记
"can能力may许可，must必须should应该，mustn't禁止needn't不必"
M4 · 推测情态动词表推测 — 确定程度从高到低核心考点

must

99%

一定、肯定

He must be at home.

may

50%

可能、也许

He may be at home.

might

30%

或许（更小可能）

He might be at home.

can't

0%

不可能

He can't be at home.

💡 口诀
"must肯定 can't不可能，may/might是也许，might比may更犹豫"
2
He ___ be at home. I just saw him in the library.
can'tmustn'tmay not
✅ just saw him in library → 不可能在家 → can't！
❌ 有证据表明不可能 → can't！
M4 · have done情态动词 + have done — 对过去的推测高考必考
结构含义例句
must have done一定做了（过去肯定）He must have missed the bus.
may/might have done可能做了（过去可能）She may have forgotten the meeting.
can't/couldn't have done不可能做了（过去不可能）He can't have finished so quickly.
should/ought to have done本该做而没做（后悔）You should have told me earlier.
needn't have done本不必做却做了You needn't have bought so much food.
could have done本可以做而没做I could have passed the exam.
3

should have toldmust have toldcould tell
✅ 本应该早告诉我(没告诉)→should have done！
❌ 本应该做而没做 → should have done！
4
The ground is wet. It ___ (rain) last night.
must have rainedshould have rainedcan have rained
✅ 地面湿→一定下过雨→must have done！
❌ 对过去的肯定推测用 must have done！
"must have肯定过"|"should have本该做"|"needn't have不必做"
