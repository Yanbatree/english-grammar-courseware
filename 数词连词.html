数词基数词 & 序数词

基数词 Cardinal

There are fifty students.

有50个学生。

1-12独立;13-19加teen;20-90加ty;⚠具体数字后不加s

📋 更多例子
two hundred people 两百人(不加s)
hundreds of people 成百上千人(加s)

序数词 Ordinal

He won first prize.

他得了第一名。

1st/2nd/3rd特殊;其余加th;ty变tieth;缩写数字+最后两字母

📋 更多例子
the twenty-first century 第21世纪
on my twelfth birthday 在我12岁生日
1
There are ___ (hundred) of birds in the sky.
hundredshundredtwo hundreds
✅ hundreds of固定→加s！
❌ hundreds of→加s；具体数字two hundred→不加s！
2
Today is my ___ (twenty) birthday.
twentiethtwentythtwentith
✅ twenty→twentieth！y变ieth。
❌ twenty变序数词→twentieth！
💡 口诀
"具体数字不加s，of短语要加s；序数词缩写看最后两字母"
数词分数 & 百分数 & 小数

分数 Fractions

Two thirds of the students are boys.

三分之二的学生是男生。

分子基数+分母序数;分子>1→分母加s

📋 更多例子
one fourth = a quarter 四分之一
three fifths of the land 五分之三的土地

百分数 %

Eighty percent of the water is clean.

80%的水是干净的。

数字+percent;of后名词定单复

📋 更多例子
50% of the work is done. 50%工作完成
50% of the students are here. 50%学生到了

小数 Decimals

3.14 → three point one four

3.14→三点一四。

point逐位读;0读zero

📋 更多例子
0.5 → zero point five 零点五
2.05 → two point zero five 二点零五
3
___ (3/5) of the earth's surface ___ (be) water.
Three fifths,isThree fifth,areThree fifths,are
✅ 3/5→分母加s→Three fifths！surface单数→is。
❌ 分子>1分母序数词加s→fifths；of后surface单数→is！
4
Seventy percent of the population ___ farmers.
areisbe
✅ population指人→复数→are！
❌ population指具体人群→复数→are！
连词并列连词 — 连接对等成分

and / both...and

He is kind and smart.

他既善良又聪明。

并列/递进;both...and谓语复数

📋 更多例子
Both Tom and Mary are coming. Tom和Mary都来
He stood up and left. 他站起来走了

but / yet / while

She is young but wise.

她年轻但聪明。

but=但是;yet=然而;while=而(对比)

📋 更多例子
He is rich, yet unhappy. 他富有但不快乐
I like tea while she likes coffee. 我喜欢茶而她喜欢咖啡

or / either...or

Hurry up, or you'll be late.

快点，否则会迟到。

选择/否则;either...or就近

📋 更多例子
Either you or he is wrong. 要么你要么他错了
Tea or coffee? 茶还是咖啡

so / for

He was tired, so he went to bed.

他累了，所以睡了。

so=所以;for=因为(补充)

📋 更多例子
It rained, so we stayed in. 下雨了所以我们待在家
He must be ill, for he looks pale. 他一定病了因为他脸色苍白

neither...nor

Neither he nor I am right.

他和我都不对。

两者都不;谓语就近

📋 更多例子
Neither the teacher nor the students were there. 老师和学生都不在
not only...but also 就近 undefined
5
___ you ___ he is right. Neither of you has thought it through.
Neither,norEither,orBoth,and
✅ neither...nor→两者都不！谓语就近→he→is。
❌ 两者都不→neither...nor！
6
Be quick, ___ you will miss the bus.
orandbut
✅ 祈使句+or→否则！固定句型。
❌ 否则→or！祈使句+or+陈述句。
连词并列句 — 简单句+连词+简单句高考改错高频

并列句公式

I like music, and she likes sports.
I like music, but she doesn't.

我喜欢音乐，她喜欢运动。

简单句, + 并列连词 + 简单句

📋 更多例子
He studies hard, so he gets good grades. 他学习努力所以成绩好
It was late, yet he kept working. 很晚了但他继续工作

⚠ 逗号不能连接两个句子！

✗ I like music, she likes sports.
✓ I like music, and she likes sports.

我喜欢音乐她喜欢运动。（错）

两个完整句子间必须有连词/分号/句号

📋 更多例子
✗逗号连两句 → 高考改错高频！ ✓加连词或变句号
undefined undefined
7
He wanted to go, ___ he was too busy.
butandso
✅ 想去 vs 太忙→转折→but！
❌ 想去但不能去→转折→but！
8
The sun was shining, ___ we decided to go out.
sobutor
✅ 阳光好→结果出去→so！因果关系。
❌ 因果关系→so！
💡 口诀
"and并列but转折，or选择so因果，逗号不能连两句"

❌ thousands of→加s！

three fourthsthree fourththird four
✅ 分子>1→分母序数词加s→three fourths！
❌ 3/4→three fourths！
3
She is not only a singer ___ also a dancer.
butandor
✅ not only...but also→固定搭配！
❌ not only...but also！
4
___ my mother ___ my father likes spicy food. They both prefer light flavors.
Neither,norBoth,andEither,or
✅ both prefer light→都不喜欢辣→neither...nor！
❌ 两者都不喜欢→neither...nor！
5
He is over seventy, ___ he still goes jogging every morning.
yetandso
✅ 七十多 vs 每天跑步→转折→yet！
❌ 年龄大但仍锻炼→yet！
6
I was about to leave ___ the phone rang.
whenandbut
✅ be about to...when→正要…这时→when！
❌ be about to do...when→固定句型！
